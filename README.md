# 💼 HAN

> Platform for job searching and hiring across CIS & EAEU countries.

---

## 🌍 About the Project

JobBridge is a job search platform designed for the CIS and EAEU region, helping connect employers and job seekers across different countries such as:

- 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan
- 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan
- 🇹🇯 Tajikistan
- 🇷🇺 Russia
- 🇧🇾 Belarus
- 🇦🇲 Armenia
- 🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan

The platform focuses on real market needs: fast hiring, remote opportunities, and cross-border employment.

---

## ⚙️ Key Features

- 🔎 Job search with filters (country, salary, experience)
- 🧑‍💼 Employer profiles
- 📄 CV upload and management
- 💬 Direct messaging between candidates and companies
- 🌐 Multilingual interface (RU / EN)
- 📊 Vacancy analytics for employers
- 📱 Mobile-friendly design

---

## 🧭 How It Works

1. User registers as candidate or employer  
2. Candidate uploads CV and applies for jobs  
3. Employer posts vacancies and reviews applicants  
4. Platform matches jobs based on skills and location  
5. Communication happens through internal chat

---

## 🎯 Problems It Solves (Reality)

- Fragmented job boards in different countries  
- Lack of unified platform across CIS/EAEU  
- Difficult cross-border hiring  
- Low transparency in vacancies  
- Slow communication between employers and candidates  

---

## 📈 Limitations (Realistic View)

- Salary data may not always be verified  
- Some employers may post outdated vacancies  
- Regional internet/payment restrictions can affect usage  
- Requires moderation for fake
